Sovereign Long-Bond Yield and Coupon Model

Stage 1 builds the fair yield and reoffer coupon for an n-year local-currency sovereign bond from its macro and risk factors. Stage 2 builds the spread of a corporate or quasi-sovereign issuer over that sovereign yield. Move any slider and the outputs update. Weights are normalised automatically, so only relative weights matter. Risk scores run from 0 (best) to 100 (worst).

Method and sources

  • Yield identity. A long yield equals the expected average of future short rates over the bond's life plus a term premium. This is the decomposition used by the New York Fed's ACM model. Liberty Street Economics
  • Sovereign credit spread. Default spread is set from the rating or CDS, then used as the base for risk premia. Damodaran, NYU Stern
  • Credit factors. Debt, debt service, reserves, growth, inflation, external balance, and institutions follow the variables that empirical panel studies of emerging-market spreads find significant. Rowland and Torres, Banco de la República
  • New-issue concession. New sovereign lines are typically priced against an interpolated reference plus a modest concession. Syndication pricing practice
  • Weights and mappings are calibrated judgements, informed by the sources above. They are not regression coefficients. Replace them with your own estimates before using the outputs for pricing.
